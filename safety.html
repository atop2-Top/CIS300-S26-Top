Hunter Safety

Stay safe, stay prepared, and hunt responsibly.

Essential Hunting Safety Tips

  • Always treat every firearm as if it is loaded.
  • Keep your muzzle pointed in a safe direction at all times.
  • Be sure of your target and what lies beyond it.
  • Wear proper eye and ear protection.
  • Use a safety harness when hunting from elevated blinds.
  • Communicate clearly with your hunting partners.
  • Check weather conditions before heading out.
  • Carry a first‑aid kit and know how to use it.
  • Let someone know your hunting location and return time.
  • Respect wildlife, landowners, and other hunters.
