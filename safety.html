Hunter Safety

Stay safe, stay prepared, and hunt responsibly.

Hunter Safety Tips

  • Always treat every firearm as if it is loaded.
  • Keep your muzzle pointed in a safe direction at all times.
  • Be sure of your target and what is beyond it.
  • Never climb or jump with a loaded firearm.
  • Wear proper safety gear, including hearing and eye protection.
  • Know your hunting area and communicate with your group.
  • Practice boat safety.
  • Always check the weather forecast.
